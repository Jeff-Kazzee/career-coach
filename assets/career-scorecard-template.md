# Career Scorecard

Scores prioritize action. They are not a grade of the person.

## Anchors

- `0` — unknown or not started
- `1` — weak and mostly unsupported
- `2` — emerging or inconsistent
- `3` — credible for the target level
- `4` — strong with repeated evidence
- `5` — unusually strong and current

Use `N/A` when a dimension does not apply. Record evidence for every score. Complete a full baseline and review monthly; change weekly scores only when evidence changes.

## Dimension prompts

### Skill strength

Can the user perform the target work at the intended level under realistic conditions? Is the capability current and repeatable?

### Proof/portfolio strength

Can an outsider verify relevant capability through work samples, outcomes, feedback, projects, credentials, or repeated performance?

### Resume clarity

Does the resume quickly communicate target fit with accurate, specific, readable evidence?

### Search consistency

Is the user finding and acting on suitable opportunities at a sustainable cadence, with records that support diagnosis?

### Interview readiness

Can the user explain fit, provide relevant examples, perform expected exercises, and evaluate the employer?

### Network/reach

Does the user have ethical routes to information, feedback, introductions, collaborators, buyers, or hiring teams beyond anonymous applications?

### Market alignment

Do target roles or buyers plausibly value the user's offer under current geography, level, pay, and access constraints?

### Professional communication

Can the user communicate clearly, accurately, appropriately, and with useful context across written and spoken work?

### Execution consistency

Does the user regularly finish the highest-value career actions within their actual capacity?

### Confidence and resilience

Can the user act, learn, and recover from setbacks without confidence becoming a prerequisite? Score behavior and recovery evidence, not personality.
