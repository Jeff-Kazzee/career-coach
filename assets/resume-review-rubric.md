# Resume Review Rubric

Score each category from 0–3. Use evidence and comments; do not collapse the review into one fake precision score.

- `0` — absent, inaccurate, or seriously obstructive
- `1` — weak or inconsistent
- `2` — credible but improvable
- `3` — strong for the target and channel
- `N/A` — not relevant
